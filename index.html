The story

Built out of necessity, 1,700 miles from the problem

"Hey — there's a hole under the siding by the back door. Something's been digging."

That text arrived while I was in San Francisco. The house is in Texas. The old version of me would have spent the evening googling pest companies, playing phone tag, and forgetting to save the receipt.

Instead, I'd spent a few weekends teaching Claude how my house works: a property fact sheet (build year, HVAC age, foundation type — the things every vendor asks), a vendor registry (who covers what, account numbers, the last tech's name), and a simple incident workflow. No code — just well-organized notes that an AI can act on.

So this time: Claude read the text, categorized it as pest/exclusion, matched it to my pest vendor, pulled our account number, and drafted two emails — one to the vendor with the photo and a request for earliest availability, one to the tenant saying help was on the way. I read both, hit send twice, and went back to my evening. Four minutes.

Since then the same system has coordinated a full tenant turnover, caught a lease clause that could have voided coverage, and kept a tax ledger that saved my accountant an hour of billable questions. The kit below is that exact system with my personal details stripped out and fill-in-the-blank templates in their place.

Questions

The things everyone asks

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The kit is folders and fill-in-the-blank documents. If you can rename a file and fill out a form, you're qualified. The start-here guide assumes you've never opened a terminal — because you shouldn't have to.

What do I need to run it?

A Claude subscription (Pro, ~$20/month — the same one millions of people already use for everyday questions) and the free Claude Desktop app. That's the entire stack. No hosting, no extra monthly fees to me, ever.

Where does my data live?

In a folder on your own computer. There is no server, no account with me, no database. Your conversations with Claude are covered by Anthropic's standard privacy terms, same as any Claude usage.

Does the AI email my tenants and vendors by itself?

No — and that's deliberate. It drafts; you review and send. It's your house, your relationships, and your name on the email. The system saves you the 40 minutes of composing, looking up account numbers, and remembering to log the expense — not the 30 seconds of judgment.

I have more than one property. Does it scale?

Yes — the structure is per-property fact sheets under one vendor registry. Several of the people I built this with manage two or three doors. If you have ten, book the setup session and we'll structure it properly from the start.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email me within 14 days for a full refund. No form, no questions.
